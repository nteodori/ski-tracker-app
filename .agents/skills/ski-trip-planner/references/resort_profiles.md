# Ski Resort Profiles & Destination Guide

This reference outlines flagship ski resorts across North America, categorized by terrain profile, vibe, snow consistency, and pass affiliation.

---

## 1. Steep & Deep / Gnarly Lines (Expert & Advanced Focus)

| Resort | State/Region | Pass | Avg Snow | Highlights & Key Vibe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jackson Hole** | Wyoming | Ikon | 458" | Legendary steeps (Corbet's Couloir), continuous vertical (4,139 ft), rugged cowboy town vibe. |
| **Snowbird / Alta** | Utah (Little Cottonwood) | Ikon | 500"+ | Deepest, lightest powder in North America; relentless fall-line terrain and chutes. |
| **Palisades Tahoe** | California | Ikon | 400"+ | Iconic cliff drops, KT-22 legendary chair, vibrant Olympic Valley vibe and lake views. |
| **Revelstoke** | British Columbia | Ikon | 450" | Most vertical in North America (5,620 ft), massive pillow lines, deep interior powder. |
| **Crested Butte** | Colorado | Epic | 300" | Extreme Limit terrain, steep cliff bands and tight chutes, funky Victorian mountain town. |
| **Telluride** | Colorado | Epic | 300" | Breathtaking San Juan peaks, hike-to Palmyra Peak, historic town connected by free gondola. |
| **Kicking Horse** | British Columbia | Epic | 295" | "Champagne powder capital of Canada", narrow couloirs, big mountain bowls. |

## 2. All-Around Cruisers & Varied Groups (Beginner to Advanced)

| Resort | State/Region | Pass | Avg Snow | Highlights & Key Vibe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vail** | Colorado | Epic | 354" | 5,300+ skiable acres, legendary Back Bowls, massive upscale European-style villages. |
| **Steamboat** | Colorado | Ikon | 314" | Famous "Champagne Powder", world-class tree skiing in aspen glades, natural hot springs. |
| **Park City Mountain** | Utah | Epic | 355" | Largest resort in the US (7,300 acres), direct ski-in/ski-out to historic Main Street, 35 mins from SLC airport. |
| **Breckenridge** | Colorado | Epic | 366" | 5 interconnected peaks, high-alpine bowls via Imperial Express, lively historic gold rush town. |
| **Sun Valley** | Idaho | Ikon | 220" | Uncrowded, immaculately groomed sustained pitch, luxury lodge amenities, sunny days. |
| **Mammoth Mountain** | California | Ikon | 400" | Huge high-elevation Sierra resort, top-tier terrain parks, long season running into June/July. |

## 3. Top Apres-Ski & Party Destinations

1. **Whistler Blackcomb (BC, Canada)**: Unmatched pedestrian village, endless bars, clubs, live DJs, and massive dual-mountain terrain.
2. **Aspen Snowmass (Colorado)**: Cloud Nine alpine champagne parties, world-class nightlife, luxury dining, 4 distinct mountains.
3. **Breckenridge (Colorado)**: Bustling main street with breweries, saloons, distillery crawls, and casual friendly energy.
4. **Heavenly (Lake Tahoe, CA/NV)**: Stateline casino nightlife, 24/7 gaming, high-energy mountain DJ cat parties at Tamarack Lodge.
5. **Vail (Colorado)**: Pepi's, Red Lion, upscale lounges, and bustling heated pedestrian streets.

## 4. Travel Logistics & Airport Proximity

* **Easiest Fly-In Access (< 1 hour transfer)**:
  - **Salt Lake City (SLC)**: 35-45 mins to Park City, Deer Valley, Snowbird, Alta, Solitude, Brighton.
  - **Jackson Hole (JAC)**: 25 mins to Teton Village / Jackson.
  - **Aspen (ASE) / Vail Eagle (EGE)**: 15-35 mins to resort bases.
  - **Reno-Tahoe (RNO)**: 45 mins to North Lake Tahoe / Palisades.
* **Denver International (DIA)**: 1.5 - 3 hours via I-70 corridor to Breckenridge, Keystone, Copper, Vail, Beaver Creek.
* **Vancouver (YVR)**: 2 - 2.5 hours scenic drive along Sea-to-Sky Highway to Whistler.
* **Calgary (YYC)**: 2 hours to Banff / Lake Louise.
